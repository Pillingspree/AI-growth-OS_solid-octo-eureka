
# AI Growth OS

> AI-powered marketing, CRM, email and automation portfolio.

AI Growth OS is an interactive portfolio and marketing systems laboratory
focused on the intersection of artificial intelligence, digital marketing,
CRM, email marketing, automation and lead generation.

The project demonstrates how I approach marketing as a connected system
rather than a collection of disconnected campaigns.

## What This Project Demonstrates

- AI-assisted marketing strategy
- Lead generation systems
- CRM architecture
- Marketing automation
- Email lifecycle design
- Lead qualification
- Customer journey mapping
- Funnel optimization
- Growth analytics
- Front-end development
